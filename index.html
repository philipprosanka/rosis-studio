About Rosi's Studio

Rosi's Studio is a small independent studio that makes simple, happy coloring books for young children. The idea is easy: friendly characters, everyday adventures and lines bold enough for a three-year-old.

The illustrations are created with AI tools. Every page is then checked by hand for clean lines, closed shapes and enough room to color before it goes to print.
